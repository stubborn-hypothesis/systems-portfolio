## Systems Engineering Philosophy
Complex hardware and regulated environments fail most often at **unverified interfaces**—between mechanical tolerances and electrical loads, between raw test data and statistical confidence, or between user needs and verification protocols. 

Trained in Industrial Engineering and hardened in Class II/III medical device R&D and manufacturing quality, I approach engineering problems through **design controls, requirements traceability, statistical modeling, and closed-loop root cause architecture**.
